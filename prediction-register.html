Prediction Register

This page is a public register of falsifiable predictions from my research on state-corporate pressure sequences and collective sensemaking. Each entry is dated, includes the conditions that would confirm or falsify it, and links to its original publication where one exists. Entries are never edited after registration; resolutions and corrections are appended with their own dates. Misses are recorded at the same prominence as hits. For a live summary, see the prediction scoreboard.
